I've got a love for examining phenomena from uncommon perspectives, cross-linking patterns, and a deep fascination for things 'bottom-up'.

Professionally, I'm a generalist designer and entrepreneur. Before co-founding a footwear brand, I specialized in brand design, UX/UI design, followed by conversion rate optimization. This was quite interesting for some time to its empirical methods of analyzing human decision-making and the directly measurable impact of design, which is often mis-regarded as, well, aesthetics. Way back, I pursued a military career, where I made it to the rank of staff seargent in the military police.

My interests are a bit all over the place:
- Metaphysics and Ontology
- Computation and artificial life (not to confuse with "AI")
- Programming languages and paradigms
- Longboarding and old-school skateboarding
- Muay Thai training, philosophy and theory
- Emacs (check out my starterkit and Emacs themes on Github)
- Traditional archery (Korean, Turkish, ...)

I care about:
- Animal welfare and animal rights
- Preserving, restoring and improving the natural environment
- Migitating anthropocentrism
- Open source and free software
