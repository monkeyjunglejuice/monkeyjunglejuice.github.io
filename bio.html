About me

I’ve got a love for examining phenomena from uncommon perspectives, cross-linking patterns, and a deep fascination for things ’bottom-up’.

Professionally: before co-founding a footwear brand, I specialized in brand design, UX/UI design, followed by conversion rate optimization. This was quite interesting for some time to its empirical methods of analyzing human decision-making and the directly measurable impact of design, which is often mis-regarded as, well, aesthetics. Way back, I pursued a military career, where I made it to the rank of staff seargent in the military police.
